In this sketch
  • The left 10–0 scale is gone.
  • Top number is the index at the start of the day; bottom is the index at the end.
  • So a day that starts at 7 and ends at 1 reads high → low; a day that starts at 1 and ends at 7 reads low → high.
  • The ribbon between them is thicker where more of the day sat, thinner where it only visited briefly.
  • Unknown days show only a question mark.
