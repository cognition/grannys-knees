# Granny's Knees Wiki

Granny's Knees is an experimental weather index for joints, with a private on-device journal.

## Wiki pages

- [How to use Granny's Knees](How-to-use)
- [How the calculations work](Calculation-logic)
- [Privacy policy](https://github.com/cognition/grannys-knees/wiki/Privacy-policy)

The index is a weather heuristic, not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or prediction of how an individual will feel. Weather and symptom ratings are shown as separate things.
