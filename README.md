# Granny's Knees

A playful weather index for your joints, and a private place to note how you feel.

**Your joints. Your weather. Your excuse.**

Granny's Knees turns the hourly forecast into an experimental 0–10 index. A higher number means less comfortable weather in the model. It is not a measurement of your pain, and it does not say the weather caused a flare.

The evidence that weather and joint symptoms line up is mixed. Correlation is not causation. Your joints may disagree with the number.

If the index is high and someone wants help moving a sofa, you have something to point at. Granny says: not today. That is a joke, not medical advice.

## Today

Pick a Canadian city. The forecast for that city stays on the phone, and you can change it later. Location permission is optional. If you decline it, city search still works.

Today shows:

- The current index, from 0 to 10, with a short band such as Mild or Rough.
- The rest of the local day. Hours that cannot be scored are left blank. They are never filled in as zero.
- The next five days as a low and a high, the way a forecast shows a temperature range.

When the forecast is missing, too old, or incomplete for this hour, the app says so. It does not invent a score.

## How you feel

You can use the index without recording anything. If you do record a change, only that report is saved. Leaving a joint alone does not store a zero.

A report can name a body part, an optional side, ache or stiffness, and a rating from 0 to 10. Zero is a real rating. You can also confirm that something is unchanged. Your reports keep the time they were written. You can delete one, export the current journal, or delete everything on the phone.

Nothing in the journal is uploaded. There is no account.

## What this is not

Granny's Knees is not a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a prediction of how you will feel. It does not coach exercise, set goals, or track fitness. The index and your own ratings stay separate. They are never added together.
